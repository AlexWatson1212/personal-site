A few answers are needed before your email can be prepared.

    Your profession, any professional membership or registration, and whether you work in person, online or both.
    Would a single, carefully designed page give you what you need for now? It can include who you are, how you work, fees, practical information and an enquiry form.
    For example several pages, online booking or a blog. A rough idea is plenty.
    Do you already own a domain name?
    Do you have recent photographs of yourself, or could you arrange some within approximately two weeks?

    Who you work with, anything in the work you particularly liked, or a question. A couple of lines is plenty.
