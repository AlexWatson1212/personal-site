{%- comment -%} Renders a legal fact from _data/legal.yml, or the original bracketed placeholder while that fact is still empty. The placeholder is not decoration: it is the signal that the page is not finished, and it must survive until the fact exists. Nothing here supplies a default value, because a default would be an invented legal fact. Usage: {% include legal-fact.html value=site.data.legal.identity.legal_name todo="registered trading name and legal status" %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fact = include.value | strip -%} {%- if fact != "" -%} {{ fact }} {%- else -%} [{{ include.todo }}] {%- endif -%}
