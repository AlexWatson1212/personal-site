Therapist Website · Checkout

Thank you. The payment process has been completed.

If the payment was taken, Stripe will email a receipt to the address you entered at checkout. Please keep it — that receipt, and your card or bank statement, are the record of payment. This page receives no payment information of any kind and cannot confirm anything on Stripe's behalf.

There is one thing to do next, and it is the thing that starts your project.

Complete the Website Content Questionnaire

What has and has not happened

Checkout is finished. Your project has not started yet.

No project has been created automatically. This website is a set of ordinary web pages — it has no account system, no automatic scheduling and no connection to Stripe. Nothing was triggered by your arriving here. Everything from this point is done by a person.

What Stripe handles

  • Taking the payment, and confirming it to you
  • Emailing your receipt — the only confirmation that counts
  • Recording the transaction, which I check in the Stripe dashboard

Not done yet

  • Your questionnaire has not been completed
  • Your materials have not been received or checked
  • No start date has been agreed
  • No build work has begun

When it officially begins

Your project officially begins once your completed Website Content Questionnaire and required materials have been received and checked, and I have confirmed the start date in writing.

That confirmation is the moment the two to four week build window starts.

Your next step

Complete the Website Content Questionnaire.

It collects everything I need in one place. You can start it now and finish it later, though the project cannot begin until it is complete.

Open the questionnaire

It prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing you type is stored by this website.

What you will need to gather

  • Your chosen design — the name of the website design from the collection
  • Final, approved page copy for each of your pages, proofread and ready to publish
  • Practice details — qualifications, memberships and registration numbers, fees, availability, how you work
  • Contact details exactly as you want them published
  • Photographs you own or are licensed to use, at the largest size you have
  • Your logo, if you have one — ideally SVG, EPS or AI, otherwise the largest PNG available
  • Colour direction — either specific colours or the design's own palette
  • Your domain name, and who it is registered with
  • Existing website address, if you have one

Large files are easiest sent as a shared folder link. If you would rather send everything by email, that is fine too.

When you will hear from me

A personal email within two working days.

Once I have seen the payment in Stripe, I will email you personally — within two working days of the checkout — confirming what I have received and answering anything outstanding. That email is written by me, not generated by this website.

Once your completed questionnaire and materials arrive, I will confirm within two working days whether anything is missing, and give you a start date in writing.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding English bank holidays.

If something has gone wrong

Paid, but heard nothing?

If more than two working days have passed with no email from me, please get in touch. Check your spam or junk folder first — replies from a small studio address sometimes land there.

hello@alexanderwatson.co.uk

Please include the name and email address you used at checkout, and the date you paid. Do not send card numbers, and you do not need to send your Stripe receipt unless I ask for it.

You can also use the contact page.

This page is not a receipt, and it is not proof of payment. It is simply the page Stripe returns you to after checkout, at a public web address that anyone could open directly. It receives nothing from Stripe, checks nothing and records nothing. Your proof of payment is the receipt Stripe emails you and the entry on your card or bank statement. If neither of those shows a payment, no payment has been taken — please get in touch before trying again.
