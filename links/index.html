Know what the project involves before you enquire.

How long does the complete project take?

Most projects take around 8–12 weeks from the Practice Clarity work to launch. The exact schedule depends on the speed of feedback, identity production, content approval and any external requirements. Your project plan confirms the dates before work begins.

Who writes the website copy?

I write the core website pages using the material developed through Practice Clarity. You provide accurate factual, clinical, legal and practical details—including qualifications, memberships, fees, policies and availability—and approve every page before launch.

How many refinement rounds are included?

The service includes two structured refinement rounds for the visual identity and two for the website. Feedback is gathered at agreed checkpoints so it can be considered as a whole. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted before it begins.

Is the logo professionally designed?

Yes. I establish the strategic and creative direction from your Practice Clarity work, then collaborate with a specialist identity designer to create the bespoke logo system. The identity is not generated or assembled from stock logo elements.

Is the website based on a template?

No. Its structure, content, visual direction and client journey are planned around your practice. It is developed as a lightweight bespoke website rather than fitted into a restrictive page builder.

Can I update the website after launch?

Yes. You receive a handover covering the parts of the site you can safely update. If you would rather not manage it yourself, optional ongoing care is available for £20 per month for minor updates. Larger changes are scoped and quoted separately.

What do I own, and what does hosting cost?

Your domain remains registered to you. After final payment you retain your approved copy, final identity files and completed website files. Managed hosting is available for £15 per month. Any third-party licences or services are identified before you approve them.

What if I decide not to continue after Practice Clarity?

There is a formal review point after Practice Clarity. If you decide not to continue before identity production begins, the project can stop there: the initial payment covers the work completed and the finished Practice Clarity document, while the second payment is not due. The project agreement records this boundary in full.

Why is the Founding Therapist Programme £795?

Alexander Watson Studio is new, and the three founding projects will become its first published client case studies. The reduced price reflects that early stage. Founding therapists receive the same complete service in exchange for an honest testimonial, constructive feedback and permission to share the agreed finished work.
