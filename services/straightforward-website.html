Revisions

Two consolidated rounds, shared by words and design.

At each revision point you review the whole website and send one complete, prioritised list. I make those changes together so the design stays coherent.

Copy changes and website changes share the same two rounds. Rewriting a paragraph, swapping a photograph and moving a section are all revisions. There is not a separate copy allowance.

A revision is not a new brief, a new design direction or an extra page. Work caused by information supplied late, a change of mind after written approval, or anything outside the agreed scope is quoted before it begins.

Feedback sent as a stream of separate messages will be held and treated as one round when it is complete.
