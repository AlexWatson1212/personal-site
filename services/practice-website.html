Feedback

Two consolidated feedback stages.

Stage one: your Practice Fundamentals and visual direction. You check every fact on the facts page, and look at the direction as a whole — the words, the logo, the colours, the lettering, the photography direction and the preview of the opening screen. Then you send one reply: either the direction feels right and I should go ahead, or it is broadly right and these specific things need adjusting. If you ask for adjustments, I make them and send the adjusted direction for you to confirm.

Stage two: the built website. You review the finished page and send one complete, prioritised list. This stage refines the direction you approved — a word, a photograph, a detail — rather than restarting with a new look.

Each stage is one consolidated set of changes. Feedback sent as a stream of separate messages is held and treated as one set when it is complete.

Corrections never use up a stage. That covers factual errors, anything I have misunderstood, and anything that departs from the direction you approved. You are not spending a stage on my mistakes.

A stage does not include a new brief or a change to a new direction after approval; additional pages; rework caused by information supplied late or changed after written approval; or features outside the one-page design.

Practice Fundamentals

Included, and not something you have to ask for.

Before anything is built, the project settles who the practice is for, what is being offered, what needs saying and what should stop being said, and how the practice should look. How much work that takes varies, but it is in the price, and deciding how much is needed is my job rather than yours.

What you receive is your Practice Fundamentals: eleven pages, including a table of every fact the website will state and a working preview of its opening screen, for you to check and approve. The same document is reissued at launch with every fact confirmed, so you can keep using it afterwards. If the work a practice needs is genuinely larger than a website project can carry, I will say so before anything starts.
