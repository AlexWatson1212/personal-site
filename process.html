01

We do not start with a logo or a website.

Therapeutic training gives you knowledge, ethical responsibility and relational skill. It does not automatically give you a clear way to describe the practice you are building or communicate what makes your work recognisably yours.

Before we discuss pages, colours or typography, we clarify the practice itself: your values, strengths, therapeutic influences, suitable clients, working style and the professional life you want to build.

That understanding becomes the foundation for your positioning, language, visual identity, professionally designed logo and website. Nothing is added simply to make the practice appear more polished. Every decision has a reason.

Positioning and messaging make the practice easier to understand.

Once your professional identity is clearer, we turn that understanding into language prospective clients can recognise.

This includes how you describe the people you work with, the experience of therapy with you, the values behind your approach and the reasons a suitable client might feel drawn towards your practice.

The aim is not to make you sound more impressive. It is to make the depth and quality of your work easier to understand without flattening it into generic marketing language.

FAQ

Questions about the process.

Is the process only for therapists at the beginning of private practice?

No. A good fit is about readiness rather than career stage. It can support a therapist preparing to launch, developing an existing practice or replacing an identity and website that no longer reflect the quality of their work.

Do I need to have chosen a niche?

No. Practice Clarity helps identify patterns in the people, needs and therapeutic relationships that naturally suit you. It creates useful focus without forcing your work into an artificial or overly narrow label.

Do I need to prepare my own copy?

No. You can bring rough notes, existing profiles or no finished wording at all. The core message and page direction grow from the strategic work rather than being expected from you at the beginning.

Is the logo professionally designed?

Yes. Your logo is developed by a specialist professional brand designer from the strategic and visual direction established through Practice Clarity. The process includes concepts, feedback and revision rounds before final files are prepared.

Is the website based on a template?

No. The website is designed and developed specifically around your practice, identity, content and client journey. It is not a pre-made therapy template with your colours and wording added.

What is different about a founding place?

Only the investment. Founding therapists receive the same complete service, scope and attention. The reduced £795 investment is offered because these projects will help refine the public process and become the studio's first published client case studies.

What happens after I make contact?

I will reply personally and ask a small number of questions about your practice, timing and what you hope to build. We can then decide whether the service is the right fit. If I do not think I am the right person to help, I will say so.
