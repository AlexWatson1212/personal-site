The process

From professional identity to a private practice people can recognise and trust.

I help therapists who are establishing or redefining their private practice clarify what distinguishes their work and translate it into positioning, language, visual identity and a website designed to earn trust.

Start a conversation Explore the full service

01

We do not start with a logo or a website.

Therapeutic training gives you knowledge, ethical responsibility and relational skill. It does not automatically give you a clear way to describe the practice you are building, identify the clients who suit you or communicate what makes your work recognisably yours.

Before we discuss pages, colours or typography, we explore the practice you are building: your values, strengths, natural ways of relating, suitable clients and the professional presence you want to establish around them.

That understanding becomes the foundation for every later decision.

04

Positioning and messaging come next.

Once your professional identity is clearer, we turn that understanding into language prospective clients can recognise.

This includes how you describe the people you work with, the experience of therapy with you, the values behind your approach and the reasons a suitable client might feel drawn towards your practice.

The aim is not to make you sound more impressive. It is to make your work easier to understand without flattening it into marketing language.

FAQ

Questions about the process.

Is the process only for therapists at the beginning of private practice?

No. A good fit is about readiness rather than career stage. It can support a therapist preparing to launch, developing an existing practice or replacing an identity and website that no longer reflect the quality of their work.

Do I need to have chosen a niche?

No. The process helps identify patterns in the people and ways of working that naturally suit you. It creates useful focus without forcing you into a narrow label.

Do I need to prepare my own copy?

No. You can bring rough notes, existing profiles or no finished wording at all. The core language develops from the strategic work rather than being expected from you at the beginning.

Can I stop after receiving the Blueprint?

Yes. The Blueprint is designed to be useful independently. There is no obligation to continue into visual identity or website development.

What happens after I make contact?

I will reply personally, ask a small number of questions about your practice and recommend the simplest useful next step. If I do not think I am the right fit, I will say so.
