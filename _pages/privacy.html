I aim to collect as little personal information as possible, and to be specific about what happens to it. Your information is never sold, and it is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Information about your own therapy clients

I do not need, want or ask for any information about the people you work with. Please do not send client names, session notes, case material, referral details, correspondence or health information — not in your website copy, not in screenshots, not in testimonials, and not in anything attached to an email or a shared folder.

If identifiable client information reaches me by accident, I will tell you, delete it and confirm that I have done so. Where you want to publish a client's words, that person's clear, informed and recorded consent is your responsibility to obtain and to hold.

What is collected, and why

1. Enquiries

If you use the enquiry form or email me, you provide your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell me about your practice. The enquiry form on this website prepares a message in your own email application; it does not transmit or store anything on this website. I use enquiries to reply to you and to provide information you have asked for.

Lawful basis: your consent, and my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact my business.

2. Stripe checkout

If you buy the Choose Your Practice Website online, the payment is taken by Stripe Payments UK, Ltd. and its group companies (“Stripe”).

  • Card details are entered on Stripe's own secure payment page. They are never entered on, sent to, or stored by this website, and I never see them.
  • Stripe collects the details set out at its checkout — at present your name, business name, billing address, email address, your chosen website design and, optionally, your current website or profile address.
  • Stripe passes me the information I need to fulfil your order: typically your name, business name, billing address, email address, the amount paid, the date and those checkout answers.
  • Stripe acts as an independent controller of the payment information you give it, under its own privacy policy at stripe.com/gb/privacy.
  • The page you are returned to after checkout receives no payment information at all, and records nothing.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract; and my legal obligation to keep accurate financial records.

3. The Website Content Questionnaire

The questionnaire runs entirely in your browser and prepares an email in your own email application. Nothing you type into it is transmitted to or stored by this website. Once you send that email, I hold your answers and the materials you attach in my email account and project files, and use them only to build and support your website.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract.

4. Contract and project administration

During a project I hold our correspondence, your approvals, the scope and terms in force, drafts and revision rounds, and the files you supplied. This is how the project is run and how either of us can later show what was agreed.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract; and my legitimate interest in keeping a record of what was agreed and delivered.

5. Payment and accounting records

Invoices, receipts, and the record of what was paid and when are kept as business records.

Lawful basis: legal obligation (tax and company record keeping).

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • correct it, if it is wrong;
  • delete it, where there is no longer a lawful reason to keep it;
  • restrict or object to a particular use of it;
  • provide it in a portable format, where that right applies;
  • withdraw consent, where consent is the basis being relied on.
