Information about your own therapy clients

I do not need, want or ask for any information about the people you work with. Please do not send client names, session notes, case material, referral details, correspondence or health information — not in your website copy, not in screenshots, not in testimonials, not in the intake form or its uploads and voice notes, and not in anything attached to an email or a shared folder.

If identifiable client information reaches me by accident, I will tell you, delete it and confirm that I have done so. Where you want to publish a client's words, that person's clear, informed and recorded consent is your responsibility to obtain and to hold.

What is collected, and why

1. Enquiries

If you use the enquiry form or email me, you provide your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell me about your practice, including your answers to the enquiry form's short questions about your work, your domain name, your photographs and your timing. The enquiry form on this website prepares a message in your own email application; it does not transmit or store anything on this website. I use enquiries to reply to you and to provide information you have asked for.

Lawful basis: your consent, and my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact my business.

2. Payment

There is no checkout on this website, and no card payment of any kind. When you have agreed the scope and the price for a Practice Identity & Website in writing, I send you an invoice and you pay it by bank transfer.

  • To raise the invoice I ask you for the name it should be made out to, a billing address, and an email address to send it to. That is all the billing information I hold.
  • No card details are involved at any point. None are entered on this website, none are sent to me, and I never see or store any.
  • The transfer itself passes between your bank and the Studio's bank. Your bank and mine each hold the information a payment carries — your name, the amount, the date and the reference — as independent controllers under their own regulatory obligations and their own privacy notices, not on my instructions.
  • I keep the invoice, and the record of what was paid and when, as a business record. Everything else I hold about your project came from you directly, by email or through the intake form.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract; and my legal obligation to keep accurate financial records.

3. The intake form

Once your place is reserved, I send you a link to an intake form. The form is provided by Tally, an online form service {% include legal-fact.html value=site.data.legal.data_protection.intake_provider todo="Confirm the intake form provider's legal entity and the role it acts in (processor), e.g. the company that operates Tally, and that its data processing terms have been accepted." %}, which holds what you type and the files you upload — photographs, documents and voice or video notes — on my behalf. The link carries a project reference so I can match your answers to your project; it does not contain your name or any other personal information. ⚖

I use your answers and files only to create and support your Practice Fundamentals and website. I copy them into my own project files, and I delete them from the form service once they are no longer needed there. {% include legal-fact.html value=site.data.legal.data_protection.intake_retention todo="Confirm the retention period actually operated for intake responses and uploads held by the form service, e.g. deleted from the form service at launch." %} ⚖

The form asks about your practice and your work, not your health. Please do not share information about your own health unless you want it taken into account, and do not send client names, session notes or anything else about the people you work with.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract.

4. Contract and project administration

During a project I hold our correspondence, your approvals, the scope and terms in force, drafts and feedback, and the files you supplied. This is how the project is run and how either of us can later show what was agreed.

Lawful basis: performance of our contract; and my legitimate interest in keeping a record of what was agreed and delivered.

5. Payment and accounting records

Invoices, receipts, and the record of what was paid and when are kept as business records.

Lawful basis: legal obligation (tax and company record keeping).

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information I hold about you;
  • correct it, if it is wrong;
  • delete it, where there is no longer a lawful reason to keep it;
  • restrict or object to a particular use of it;
  • provide it in a portable format, where that right applies;
  • withdraw consent, where consent is the basis being relied on.

Email {{ cfg.support_email }} and I will respond within one month. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or by telephone on 0303 123 1113.
