The Practice Clarity pathway

Three stages. Nine useful principles.

Each stage contains three short principles. Start at the beginning or go directly to the decision in front of you.

Stage one

Understand what your practice already communicates.

Before promoting the work, notice what somebody encounters and what helps them stay.

01

The Mirror Principle

Your website can only reflect the understanding, personality and values already present within your practice.

Read principle →

02

The Waiting Room Principle

Your online presence begins shaping familiarity and trust before somebody is ready to contact you.

Read principle →

03

The Threshold Principle

Your website should reduce enough uncertainty that somebody feels safe enough to stay and look more closely.

Read principle →

Stage two

Express the practice in a way the right person can recognise.

Make the work understandable and recognisably yours without turning it into a performance.

04

The Recognition Principle

The right clients respond when your language helps them recognise their experience.

Read principle →

05

The Homepage Principle

Your homepage should quickly help the right person understand whether they are in the right place and where to go next.

Read principle →

06

The About Principle

Reveal enough of the therapist for somebody to imagine beginning a conversation.

Read principle →

Stage three

Connect the whole experience so the next step feels clear.

Remove unnecessary complexity between a first impression and a genuine enquiry.

07

The Simplicity Principle

Give every page one clear responsibility and remove what does not help.

Read principle →

08

The Consistency Principle

Help your website, imagery and communication feel like one practice.

Read principle →

09

The Enquiry Principle

Make the next step feel simple enough for the right person to take.

Read principle →
