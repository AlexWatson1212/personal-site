# Post-launch list

Things found during the Minimal Launch V2 implementation that are desirable and
were deliberately **not** done, because they are optimisation rather than launch
preparation. Ten items, in rough order of value. Nothing here blocks taking the
first £500.

1. **Publish Stillpoint as the fifth concept.** It is otherwise excellent and
   deferred for one defect: a live Netlify form on a fictional therapy practice.
   Fix is small — see `CONCEPT-PUBLICATION-ASSESSMENT.md`.

2. **Decide whether Website Care should include a small allowance of content
   changes.** The published position is that changes after the first thirty days
   are quoted. That is honest and now stated plainly on `/service/`, but a
   therapist changing her fee once a year being quoted each time may cost more in
   goodwill than the work is worth. A commercial decision, not a copy fix.

3. **A client-facing project record.** One private page per client showing the
   stages with dates. Turns "there is always a visible next step" into a link.
   Wait until there is a project to record.

4. **The continuity note and the Care runbook.** Where the code lives, how it
   deploys, what a different developer would need — and how each Care claim is
   actually delivered. Needed before the first client, not before launch.

5. **A stated method for the proportionate refund figure.** The terms promise to
   show how it was worked out. Decide the method — stage values or a day rate —
   before it is ever needed under pressure.

6. **A shared concept-notice component and a deploy runbook** across the concept
   repositories, so the disclosure and the publishing steps are identical rather
   than reimplemented per concept.

7. **North & Vale V2.** Already diagnosed and commissioned. A craft project; it
   will be more enjoyable than anything on this list, which is the risk.

8. **Rebuild the Practice Clarity and Master Templates PDFs.** They still name
   retired typefaces and have no editable source. Real debt, not launch debt.

9. **A transient contrast dip during the home hero entrance animation.** With the
   animation settled, every route passes WCAG AA with zero violations. Mid-
   animation, the price note briefly measures 3.85:1. Harmless, worth tidying.

10. **`/what-happens/`, only if evidence justifies it.** Not built. If people ask
    the same process question twice before enquiring, that is the evidence. Build
    it then, from real questions.
