Nothing on the public site links here and the page is kept out of search four ways: `noindex: true` renders the robots meta tag, `sitemap: false` keeps it out of sitemap.xml, robots.txt disallows /client/, and netlify.toml sends X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow for /client/*. None of that is authentication. Anyone with the address can open and submit this form; it is unguessable, not protected. Do not describe it as secure. NETLIFY FORMS. Detection happens at deploy time against the built HTML, so every control below is rendered by Jekyll into the page whether or not the step it belongs to is the one on screen. The JavaScript only hides steps; it never creates, moves or removes a field. Three things have to agree or the submission is rejected as a 404 or lands under the wrong form: · name="practice-discovery" on the
· the hidden form-name input carrying the same value · netlify-honeypot naming a field that actually exists All three come from _data/practice_discovery.yml so they cannot drift apart. PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT. Without JavaScript this is one long, ordinary form: every fieldset visible, native `required` doing the validation, one submit button. assets/js/practice-discovery.js turns it into eleven steps, takes over validation (setting novalidate so the browser stops competing with it), and reveals the Previous/Continue controls that are hidden in the markup — hidden because without the script they would do nothing. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pd = site.data.practice_discovery -%} {%- assign step_count = pd.steps | size -%}

Client process

Practice Discovery

This questionnaire is the starting point for your Practice Fundamentals, identity and website.

It gives me an opportunity to understand not only what your practice offers, but how you think, how you work and how you want people to experience you.

You don’t need perfectly written answers. Honest thoughts, unfinished ideas, links, screenshots and instinctive reactions are all useful, and a half-formed sentence often tells me more than a careful paragraph.

Only the questions marked Required must be completed — there are ten, and most of them take seconds. Everything else is an invitation, not an obligation. Some questions will matter a great deal to your practice and others will not apply at all; answer the ones that feel alive to you and skip the rest without a second thought. Where something needs further exploration, we will talk about it properly rather than solve it here.

Most people will need around 35–50 minutes. You may find it helpful to look through the questionnaire first and gather any useful links, images or existing materials before beginning.

Your answers will remain in the form while you move between sections, but this first version does not securely save an unfinished form for later. Please complete and submit it in one sitting.

There are two places to attach a file, and each one takes a single file. Keep the combined size below 7 MB. If you have several files, or anything larger, share a folder link instead — there is a field for it — or email them to me.

Please don’t include confidential or identifiable information about individual clients, in what you write or in what you upload. Never enter passwords or account recovery information.

Your responses will be used to develop your Practice Fundamentals, identity and website. Please share information about your own practice only, and do not include confidential information about clients. How your information is handled.
