Your intake form.

Thank you for reserving your place. This form is where I learn about your practice, and everything I make for you starts from what you tell me here.

Your project reference is . It is added to the form for you, so there is nothing to copy.

Before you start

Four things worth knowing.

Rough notes are welcome
Bullet points, half-formed thoughts and voice notes are all useful, and often more useful than finished sentences, because they sound like you. Nothing needs to be polished.
The facts need to be exact
Questions labelled Fact needed before publishing cover what your website will state as fact: fees, qualifications, memberships, access and similar. Please check each one. “I don’t know yet” is a perfectly good answer, and I will never fill a gap with a guess.
You don’t have to answer everything
Core question marks the seven questions that help me most; please answer at least four of them, typed or as a voice note. Anything labelled Optional context or Optional reference or upload is an invitation, not a requirement.
Take as many sittings as you like
It usually takes 60 to 90 minutes in total. Your answers are saved as you go, as long as you come back on the same device and in the same browser, and not in a private window.

Please do not include anything about your own clients. No client names, session notes, initials, or details that could identify a current or former client, even to themselves — not in your answers, your files or your voice notes. Where a question asks about the people you work with, describe patterns, never individuals.

Please don’t share information about your own health either, unless you want it taken into account.

Photographs

Worth starting now.

The most common delay is waiting for photographs. The minimum is a natural photograph of you, with several usable variations (at least one looking towards the camera), and the room you work in if you see people in person. If you don’t have these yet, arranging them while you fill in the form keeps everything moving.

What happens next

Everything after this is in writing.

  1. I read everythingWithin two working days of receiving the form. If anything is unclear, you’ll get one short message of questions.
  2. Your Practice FundamentalsAbout a week later: your words, your practical facts, your visual identity, and a working preview of your website’s opening screen.
  3. You approve the directionYou check the facts and either approve the direction or send one set of adjustments. This is the first of two feedback stages.
  4. The buildOnce you’ve approved it, I send the {{ cfg.balance_display }} balance invoice, and I build the website when it’s paid.
  5. Review and launchYou send one set of feedback on the built website. Then I make the corrections, connect your domain and launch.
