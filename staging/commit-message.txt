Recompose the site around one product, one add-on and one stylesheet

Commercial architecture
  Therapist Website £995, including the first twelve months of Website Care.
  Practice Clarity becomes a £500 add-on (£1,495 together) rather than a
  separate, larger product. Custom work is quoted individually. The two-route
  structure, the ~£2,000 bespoke website and the £290 annual Care price are
  retired, and scripts/qa.mjs fails the build if any of them reappear.

  Service terms clause 12 is rewritten: Care is now a twelve-month obligation
  inside the price, so it states what it is, what it is not, what happens
  after twelve months, and thirty days' notice before removal. Care no longer
  claims backups or uptime monitoring, neither of which this setup provides.

Design system
  assets/css/studio.css replaces main.css (14,899 lines, 43 dated override
  passes), visual-system.css, catalogue-refresh.css, links.css and thirteen
  generated bundles: 19,094 lines and 1,215 !important declarations become
  3,255 lines and none, enforced by the build. CSS over the wire drops from
  23.0 KB per page to 13.1 KB site-wide.

  Motion has one grammar — 14px, 620ms, one curve — with reduced motion
  expressed by flipping the tokens rather than a blanket reset. Scroll reveals
  are CSS scroll-driven animations behind @supports; no animation library.

Information design
  The collection is now data (_data/collection.yml + _includes/plate.html):
  each direction is drawn as a miniature of its own home page, with its own
  layout personality. Prose was replaced by a service anatomy, a three-column
  ledger, a drawn Practice Clarity pathway and a tailoring diff. Running prose
  is down 18%.

Verification
  64/64 unit checks, 0 axe violations across 31 routes x 4 viewports, 0 browser
  QA failures, 0 typography failures, 0 target-size failures. Home LCP 236ms,
  CLS 0.
