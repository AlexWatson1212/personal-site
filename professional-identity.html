01 · The gap

You can feel grounded in the room and unclear everywhere else.

Training develops therapeutic knowledge, ethical awareness and relational skill, but it does not always teach you how to describe the qualities you bring into the room, recognise the clients who naturally suit you or decide what your practice should communicate outside it.

That gap can leave the quality of your work difficult to recognise. Your website, directory profile or visual identity may communicate only a fraction of what working with you may actually feel like.

Practice Clarity notices what is already taking shape: the values you return to, the language that feels natural, the relationships in which you work best and the qualities people experience in your presence.

03 · How it works

A defined process for thoughtful work.

The Practice Clarity stage normally takes around two weeks and combines private written reflection with one focused conversation.

It is a strategic service, not personal therapy or clinical supervision. The purpose is to make grounded decisions about your professional practice and how it is communicated.

  1. 01

    Private written questionnaire

    Complete the reflective questions in your own time, with room to think rather than perform an answer in a call.

  2. 02

    One 90-minute online working session

    We explore patterns, tensions and decisions that need more attention, using your written responses as the starting point.

  3. 03

    Draft Practice Clarity document

    I synthesise the work into a structured draft and send it within ten working days of the session.

  4. 04

    One consolidated refinement round

    You review the document in private and return one set of considered feedback before the foundation is approved.

FAQ

Questions before beginning.

Do I need to have chosen a niche?

No. The process helps you notice patterns in the clients, concerns and relationships that naturally suit you. The aim is useful focus, not an artificial label.

What if my practice is still developing?

That is expected. You do not need a finished niche or fixed identity. The process creates a truthful foundation for where you are now and enough direction for what comes next.

Is this the same as personal branding?

It is related, but the work begins earlier. Before deciding how to present your practice, we clarify what the practice genuinely needs to express.

Is Practice Clarity sold separately?

No. It is delivered as the strategic first stage of the studio service so the later work can grow from the same approved foundation.

How long does the complete project take?

Most projects are planned across approximately eight to twelve weeks. The exact schedule depends on feedback, content readiness and the complexity of the website, and is agreed before work begins.

Can I stop after Practice Clarity?

Yes. Practice Clarity is a formal approval point. If you decide not to continue before identity production begins, the initial payment covers the completed strategic work and document; the second payment is not due. The precise terms are set out in the project agreement.
