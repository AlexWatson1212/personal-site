What I make

An identity and a website, built from understanding the practice.

Most therapist websites start with the website: a template, a colour, a homepage headline. I start with the practice — who you work with, how you work, what someone needs to understand before they get in touch — and write that down as your Practice Fundamentals. The identity and the website are built from it, so the practice looks and sounds like itself on the website, on a directory profile and on anything you print.

It is one complete, carefully designed page with your words, your fees, the practical information and an enquiry route that suits a therapy practice. You own it, your domain stays in your own name, and the files are handed over at the end.
