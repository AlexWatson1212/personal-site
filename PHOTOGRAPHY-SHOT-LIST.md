## Capture guidance

- Use soft window light and the real working desk.
- Keep paper text legible enough to feel authentic but not so sharp that confidential notes can be read.
- Use approved dummy or personal-practice material only; never include client or clinical information.
- Photograph the work as it is genuinely used: pencil marks, revision notes and print tests are valuable.
- Keep hands optional and faces out of frame unless the image is intentionally a portrait.
- Avoid props added only to signal “calm”: tea cups, stones, candles, plants and empty journals.
- Shoot each arrangement once in landscape 4:3, once in 16:10 and once in portrait 4:5.
- Export WebP at 80–85 quality, remove location metadata and keep each final file below roughly 250 KB where practical.
