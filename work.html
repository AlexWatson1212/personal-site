The collection

Six practices. Six answers that look nothing like each other.

Six fictional therapy practices, each given a complete website and, published beside it, the strategic work that produced it. Every conclusion sits next to the design decision it caused, so you can check the second against the first.

They are not templates and not a house style with six colourways. Notice which one you keep returning to — that is a real signal, because these six have almost nothing in common.

What every case has behind it

A live website, and the reasoning that produced it.

Each of the six is deployed and can be visited, page by page, on a phone if you want to see it there. Each has a strategic document published in full and free to read, in which every design decision is set against the conclusion it came from. Where a document and a build disagreed, the build changed and the document says so.

Most of the people arriving on a therapist’s website are on a phone, and some of them are not calm. The phone view of each of these is not the desktop one stacked up: the hierarchy is re-decided, the type re-set, and the thing a distressed reader needs first is moved to where they will find it. All six are built to WCAG 2.2 AA.
