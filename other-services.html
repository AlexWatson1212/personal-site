Other services

Sometimes it is one part that needs attention, not the whole practice.

Not every practice needs a new identity and website. Sometimes what helps is a second pair of eyes, a clearer directory profile, one page that says more plainly what you do, or one part of how the practice appears online put right.

Most of my work is the complete Practice Identity & Website, and if the whole practice needs describing, that is still where I would start. Alongside it I take on smaller pieces of work like these, where they are a good fit and I can do them properly.

What I can help with

Five smaller pieces of work.

Each one is bounded and agreed in writing before it starts. None of them needs the complete service first, and none of them is a cut-down version of it.

01

Counselling profile review

For Counselling Directory, Psychology Today and similar listings.

A directory profile is often the first thing a prospective client reads, and it is usually written once and rarely looked at again. I read it the way the right client would.

  • A plain assessment of clarity, positioning and tone
  • What may be getting in the way of the right person
  • A clearer structure for the profile
  • Key sections rewritten where that helps

02

SEO setup and assessment

The foundations, measured, then reviewed.

{%- comment -%} 25 September 2026, on Alexander's instruction. A bounded setup and a later review, not ongoing SEO: understand, set up, improve, measure, wait, review. No ranking promise, no retainer, no monthly work. See DECISION-REGISTER.md, "SEO setup and assessment". {%- endcomment %}

Most practice websites do not need someone constantly changing them. They need the foundations in place, a clear view of what is actually happening, and someone who can make sense of it. I start with what you want the website to do, and which searches really matter to the practice.

  • The foundations checked: whether Google can find every page, and the titles, descriptions and structure it reads, with local relevance where you serve a particular area
  • A small number of useful improvements, rather than a long list of changes
  • A note of where things start, from Google Search Console where that makes sense, and a sensible way of measuring what happens next
  • A review after an agreed period — usually around three months — of what Google is actually showing, and a recommendation for the next step

You will also have a simple record of the starting point, what changed and what we are waiting to learn.

Search takes time, and nobody can promise a ranking, so I do not. The wait is what gives the review something real to read. The setup can usually be quoted as one defined piece of work; the review is booked separately, and any further changes it points to are agreed as separate work. If that sounds like what your website needs, tell me what you need.

03

Website improvements

For a website you already have, that does not need rebuilding.

Often a site is mostly right, and one part of it is not doing its job. Whether I can work on it depends on how and where it is built.

  • A new page, or an existing one restructured
  • Clearer wording, and clearer next steps for a visitor
  • A simpler route from reading to getting in touch
  • Mobile, usability and technical tidying

04

Brand and practice materials

Print and digital pieces that belong to the same practice.

The things that sit alongside a website, and should look as though they come from the same place.

  • Small refinements to an existing identity
  • Business cards, leaflets and referral materials
  • Profile and social graphics
  • Print-ready files and artwork a supplier can use

05

Focused messaging help

Practical recommendations, not a strategy document.

An outside view of how the practice is presented, and what is worth changing.

  • How the practice currently comes across
  • Who you are trying to reach, made clearer
  • Messaging refined into language you would actually use
  • The route from profile or website to an enquiry
