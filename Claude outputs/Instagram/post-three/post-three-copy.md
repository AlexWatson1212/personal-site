## Caption

Another one from reading therapist profiles.

The words people use to describe themselves are probably true. Warm, compassionate, empathic. But they appear on so many profiles that they don't tell you much about this particular person.

What seemed to tell me more was quieter: how someone described a first session, or what they said happens when you don't know where to start.

I noticed it partly because I'll need to write my own profile once I qualify, and I'm fairly sure those same three words would be the first ones I reached for.

An accurate profile won't appeal to everyone. I don't think it's meant to.
