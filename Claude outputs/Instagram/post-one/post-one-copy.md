## Caption

I've been working on this for a long time and I've barely mentioned it on here, so this is me starting to.

I've worked in marketing for about 15 years: my own businesses, social channels for other businesses, and helping people with how they come across publicly. I'm now in my second year of training as a counsellor. Over time those two things have come together into Alexander Watson Studio, which works with therapists and counsellors in private practice.

When I started, I thought I was building websites for therapists. Developing the process showed me that the important work usually starts earlier: who the practice is for, how you work, how the practice describes itself, and how it should sound and look. The website comes from that.

I've developed and tested the process through three detailed fictional practices. They're made up, and the site says so clearly. I don't have real client case studies to show yet. Working with real therapists is the next stage.

I'll share more of the work and thinking on here as I go.

There's more on the website if you'd like to have a look. Link in my bio.
