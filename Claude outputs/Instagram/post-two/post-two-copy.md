## Caption

I've been going through one therapist's website and directory profiles recently, and this was the thing that stood out.

Everything on them was accurate and useful. I could tell you how she works, what she works with and how long she's been practising. What I couldn't tell you was what she'd be like in a first session.

I don't think a profile can fully answer that, and it shouldn't promise it. But a sentence or two might help more than you'd expect. Something like "I tend to ask quite a few questions early on", or "I'll mostly listen at first", tells someone something about sitting in the room with you that another approach on the list probably won't.

It's one of the things I'm paying more attention to at the moment.
