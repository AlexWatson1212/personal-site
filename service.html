The project map

Four stages, each with a clear outcome.

You always know what is happening, what I need from you, what you will receive and when approval is required. Practice Clarity + Website covers all four stages, Website + Identity begins at stage two, and Website Launch focuses on stage four.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Practice Clarity

    Establish the strategic foundation before visual or technical decisions are made.

    What happens

    Written discovery, review and focused clarification of your practice, values, services, suitable clients and direction.

    You receive

    A completed Practice Clarity Document covering the strategic, verbal and creative foundation of the project.

    Approval point

    The foundation is reviewed and approved before copy and visual direction are developed.

  2. 02

    Articulate

    Positioning & copy

    Turn the strategic foundation into clear language and a useful route through the website.

    What happens

    Positioning, core messages, tone of voice, page structure and the main client journey are developed.

    You receive

    Agreed website architecture and core page copy ready to guide design and development.

    Approval point

    You review factual details, tone and the overall communication direction before design begins.

  3. 03

    Express

    Brand direction

    Translate the strategy into a distinctive visual system with a clear rationale.

    What happens

    Creative direction, a tailored wordmark treatment, colour, typography and supporting visual decisions are developed.

    You receive

    A Brand System Sheet, wordmark files, colour and typography guidance, and a practical visual system.

    Approval point

    Two consolidated refinement rounds are included before the direction is approved.

  4. 04

    Build

    Website & launch

    Bring the strategy, language and brand direction together in a complete digital home for the practice.

    What happens

    Bespoke design and development, responsive testing, accessibility review, foundational SEO and launch preparation.

    You receive

    The completed website, launch support, handover and ownership of the agreed files.

    Approval point

    Two consolidated website refinement rounds, including copy feedback, are included before final approval and launch.

FAQ

Practical questions before you enquire.

How long does a project take?

Practice Clarity + Website usually takes around 8–12 weeks from the beginning of Practice Clarity to launch. Website Launch and Website + Identity are shorter, and the schedule is confirmed before booking. Every plan assumes feedback and materials are returned within five working days.

How do we communicate during the project?

Email is the default, with clear written questions, recommendations and decisions kept in one place. Calls are available when genuinely useful, scheduled in advance, and the important outcomes are confirmed in writing.

Who writes the website copy?

With Website Launch you supply your core content and I shape it for the page. With Website + Identity I also help shape the message, and with Practice Clarity + Website the core pages are written from your Practice Clarity material. In every case you provide and approve factual, clinical, legal and practical details such as qualifications, memberships, fees, policies and availability.

How many refinement rounds are included?

Two consolidated refinement rounds are included for brand direction and two for the website. Website rounds include copy feedback. A round is one consolidated set of feedback, sent in a single message. Additional work is scoped and quoted before it begins.

What is included in the visual direction?

Visual direction is part of Website + Identity and Practice Clarity + Website. It covers a tailored visual direction, a logo or wordmark treatment where appropriate, colour, typography and a coherent professional identity, set out in a Brand System Sheet with practical guidance for using it.

Is the website based on a template?

No. Every service, including Website Launch, is planned around your practice and developed as a lightweight, fast website rather than fitted into a restrictive page builder.

How does payment work?

Projects are normally paid in two stages: 50% to book your place in the schedule and 50% before launch. The written agreement confirms the exact scope, responsibilities, timing and payment schedule before any work begins.

What do I own after launch?

Your domain remains registered to you. After final payment you retain your approved copy and completed website files, along with your Brand System Sheet and wordmark files where identity work is included. Any third-party licences are identified before approval.

Can you maintain the website afterwards?

Yes. The first year of Website Care is included. After that, it is £29 per month or £290 per year, with larger changes scoped and quoted separately.

How does the Founding Client Offer work?

Three Website Launch places are available at £795 rather than £995, including the first year of Website Care. In return, I may show the finished public website as a portfolio example after launch. Candid feedback is welcome, but a testimonial is optional and would only be published with your approval.
