FAQ

Practical questions before you enquire.

How long does Practice Clarity Studio take?

Most projects take around 8–12 weeks from the beginning of Practice Clarity to launch. The confirmed project plan accounts for feedback, identity production, content approval and external requirements.

How do we communicate during the project?

Email is the default, with clear written questions, recommendations and decisions kept in one place. Calls are available when genuinely useful, scheduled in advance, and the important outcomes are confirmed in writing.

Who writes the website copy?

I write the core website pages using the material developed through Practice Clarity. You provide and approve factual, clinical, legal and practical details such as qualifications, memberships, fees, policies and availability.

How many refinement rounds are included?

Two structured refinement rounds are included for the professional identity and two for the website. Additional work is scoped and quoted before it begins.

Is the logo professionally designed?

Yes. I establish the strategic and creative direction through Practice Clarity, then collaborate with a specialist identity designer to produce the bespoke logo system.

Is the website based on a template?

No. The structure, copy, visual direction and client journey are planned around your practice and developed as a lightweight bespoke website rather than fitted into a restrictive page builder.

What if I decide not to continue after Practice Clarity?

There is a formal review point after Practice Clarity. If you stop before identity production begins, the first payment covers the work completed and the finished document; the second payment is not due.

What do I own after launch?

Your domain remains registered to you. After final payment you retain your approved copy, final identity files and completed website files. Any third-party licences are identified before approval.

Can you maintain the website afterwards?

Yes. Managed hosting is available for £15 per month. Optional ongoing care for minor updates is £20 per month, while larger changes are scoped and quoted separately.
