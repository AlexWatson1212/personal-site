Practice Clarity

The other job — and most therapists do not need it.

The website service turns your answers into a website. Practice Clarity produces the answers.

The intake is part of the £995, and reading it properly is my job. If what you send is clear enough to build from, I build. If it shows the practice itself has not been decided yet, I say so — and this is what deciding it costs.

You are not asked to work out which of those you are. If you can already say who you work with and what you offer, the £995 website is a complete piece of work and I would rather you kept the £500.

Practice Clarity £500

Agreed in writing and invoiced separately, before a website starts or on its own. The written Practice Direction is yours to keep, whether or not a website follows.

  1. Practice

    What is actually being offered here, in plain terms, and what is not?

  2. People

    Who is this for — and, just as usefully, who is it not for?

  3. Position

    What is distinctive about it, said without claiming anything you cannot stand behind?

  4. Message

    What should somebody understand within seconds, and what language fits you well enough to say out loud?

  5. Expression

    How the practice should feel, what to emphasise, what to remove, and how the services are organised.

The output is a short written Practice Direction covering those five. It is useful on a directory profile, in a referral conversation or on a leaflet. If a website follows, it is built from that — which is mostly a matter of the design having something settled to express.

Questions I am asked most

Do I own the website?

Yes. On full payment the finished website made for your practice is yours to keep, host and move, and your domain stays in your own account. The underlying design system stays mine, licensed to you permanently for one website for your own practice.

What if I choose the wrong direction?

Before the intake is complete, one change of direction is free. After the build has started it is a new brief rather than a revision, and is quoted.

Can you write it for me?

I edit and set what you write, which for most therapists is the right amount of help: the words still sound like you. Writing from a blank page is Practice Clarity work.

Will you follow my professional body's rules?

Designations are set exactly as BACP, UKCP, NCPS or your own body permits, and nothing goes on the page you cannot stand behind. I do not give regulatory advice, and the published website remains your responsibility.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

No. The intake is in plain language, the design decisions are mine, and if you would rather not touch it at all, that is what Care is for.

Can I pause, or get a refund?

A project that goes quiet is held for sixty days, then treated as dormant. Refunds depend on how far the work has gone; the position is set out in full on the cancellations and refunds page.

Why is this cheaper than a bespoke website?

Because the exploration has already happened. What you are buying is the tailoring, the build and the care — not three weeks of concept work.
