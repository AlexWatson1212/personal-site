What a revision means

Two clear rounds, not a running stream of changes.

At each revision point, you review the website as a whole and send one complete, prioritised list. I make the agreed changes together so the design stays coherent.

A revision is not a new brief, a new website direction or extra pages. Changes caused by information supplied late, a change of mind after approval, or work beyond the agreed scope are quoted before I begin them.

This protects your budget and keeps the project moving.
