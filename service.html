Most £995 projects run three to five weeks from a completed intake. The pace is set by how quickly content and feedback come back.

What a revision means

Two clear rounds, not a running stream of changes.

At each revision point you review the website as a whole and send one complete, prioritised list. I make the agreed changes together so the design stays coherent.

A revision is not a new brief, a new direction or extra pages. Work caused by content supplied late, a change of mind after approval, or anything beyond the agreed scope is quoted before I begin it.

This protects your budget and keeps the project moving.

Before you ask

The questions I am asked most.

Do I own the website?

Yes, outright, once the final payment clears. Your domain and hosting stay in your own accounts rather than mine, so nothing is held hostage if you move on.

What if I choose the wrong direction?

You can change your chosen direction once, free, before I begin tailoring it. After that a change means starting the design again, so it is quoted.

I am not confident with technology.

You do not need to be. There is nothing to install, configure or maintain. If you want to edit pages yourself afterwards I will show you how, and if you would rather not, Website Care covers small changes.

Can you write my copy?

At £995 I edit and set what you write. Substantial copywriting belongs to route two, where the words come out of the clarity work rather than a blank page.

Will it follow my professional body's rules?

Designations are set exactly as BACP, UKCP, NCPS or your own body permits, and no claim goes on the page that you cannot stand behind. Client testimonials are not used — some bodies prohibit them outright.

What if I need to pause?

Tell me. Projects pause for illness, caseload and life. The scope and price are held; I simply pick it back up when you are ready.
