# Handover — what it is, and how to do one

Alexander Watson Studio · internal · written 5 September 2026

"Handover" is promised on the Therapist Website page, in the process list, and in
clause 12 of the service terms. Until now it was never defined anywhere, which meant a
client could not tell what she would receive and the Studio had no fixed answer. This
file is that answer. The public definition in clause 12 is derived from it and the two
must not drift apart.

**Not published.** `docs` is in the `exclude` list in `_config.yml`.

---

## When a handover happens

Three moments, and they are the same pack each time:

1. **At launch**, as a matter of course. Handover is not a thing that happens when a
   relationship ends; it happens when the job finishes.
2. **Whenever the client asks**, at any point afterwards.
3. **Before the website is removed** from Studio infrastructure if Website Care is not
   continued — clause 12 gives at least thirty days' notice, and the pack goes with the
   notice, not after it.

---

## The pack

One folder, sent as a single archive, with a plain README at the top.

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `source/` | The complete source of her website — templates, styles, content files, and the repository history if it is a git project |
| `build/` | The built static files a host needs to serve, exactly as deployed |
| `content/` | Her copy, her images at original size, and the logo files she supplied or that were made for her |
| `README.md` | How to deploy the built files, how to point the domain, and how to rebuild from source |
| `dependencies.md` | Every third-party service the site touches, what it costs, and whose account it is on |

Nothing in the pack requires the Studio to stay involved for it to work.

## The seven things a client actually asks

**What do I own?** Her content and practice information are hers throughout. The
finished website made for her practice is hers on full payment. The underlying design
system and code are licensed to her for that one website — clause 13. Say this plainly
rather than saying "it's yours" and leaving the licence to be discovered later.

**Who controls the domain?** She does. Register it in her name, on her account, from the
start, and never in the Studio's. This is the single decision that makes every later
question easy, and it is worth insisting on even when a client would rather you handled
it. If she has asked the Studio to buy it for her, transfer it to her account before
launch, not at handover.

**Where does the code live?** In a repository the Studio holds during the project. At
handover she receives the full source, and the history with it. If she wants the
repository itself moved to her own account, that is a transfer, not a rebuild, and it
costs nothing.

**Can I get into the live site?** There is no login, because there is no CMS. That is
disclosed before purchase and is part of what keeps the price at £995. Changes are an
email; the files are hers to hand to anyone.

**What happens if I stop Website Care?** Thirty days' notice, the pack, and the site
comes off Studio infrastructure at the end of it. Nothing is withheld and nothing is
deleted without the notice having been given.

**What still depends on somebody else?** The domain registrar, the host, font licences,
any stock image licences, and the email provider if one is involved. Each is listed in
`dependencies.md` with the cost and the account holder. Those are supplied under their
own terms — clause 12.

**What if you disappear?** Everything above is designed so that the answer is "nothing
of yours is trapped". The pack is deliberately a set of ordinary files and written
instructions, not an export from a system only the Studio can run.

## Standing rules

- **Her domain, her account, from day one.** No exceptions, however convenient.
- **Handover at launch, not on request.** A client who already has the pack never has to
  ask for it, and never wonders what would happen if she did.
- **Never a promise of an account or a portal.** The offer is files and instructions, and
  the public wording says so.
- **If the pack changes, clause 12 changes with it.** The public definition is the
  binding one.
