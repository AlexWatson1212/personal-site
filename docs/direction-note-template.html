Direction Note — how to fill this in

Duplicate the file, replace every [bracket], delete nothing else. Then Print → Save as PDF, landscape, margins None, background graphics on. This panel does not print.

  • Word limits are the design. §1 70 words · §2 40 · §3 45 · §4 four to six lines · §6 five pages · §7 two to three lines. Over the limit means the thinking is not finished.
  • Her words, not yours. Every noun for what she does comes from her intake. If she wrote “long-term work”, it does not become “depth-oriented practice”.
  • No mockup, ever. A picture here turns her approval into an early Round One and the document stops working.
  • Only what moved. A tailoring line reading “typography unchanged” is filler — delete the row.
  • If §3 does not fit, say so here and propose the alternative. That is cheaper than building to the wrong one.
  • If a field pulls toward personas, values, competitors or a moodboard, stop: the practice may genuinely need Practice Clarity, which is a conversation, not a section.
  • Send as a PDF attached to an email whose body carries the approval question in plain text, so it can be answered from a phone.
