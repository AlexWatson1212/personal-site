Two worked examples

What it looks like when all nine are applied to two very different practices.

Sofia Marin and Maya Bennett are fictional practices with finished concept websites. The strategic work behind each is published in full, set out so that every conclusion sits beside the design decision it produced — which is the only honest way to show that the thinking did any work. The two sites have almost nothing in common, which is rather the point.

Fourteen pages, free to read, and honest about its own order: the website existed first, and the document reconstructs the foundation that could credibly have produced it. It separates what the site demonstrably does from what that suggests about the practice behind it.
