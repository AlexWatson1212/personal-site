A calm way to work

Communication is written-first: clear questions, considered recommendations and decisions kept in one place. Calls are available where genuinely useful and scheduled in advance, but are never expected as the default.

Projects are normally paid in two stages: 50% to book your project and 50% before launch. The exact scope, timing, responsibilities and payment schedule are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Website Care after year one

After the included first year, Website Care can continue at £29 per month or £290 per year. It covers secure hosting, maintenance, backups, monitoring and small content amendments. Larger work is scoped and quoted separately.
