How the £995 route runs

Six steps, and you make two of them.

  1. 1

    You choose a direction

    Browse the collection and pick the one closest to the practice you want people to recognise.

  2. 2

    You complete the intake

    A focused written form about your practice, your content and your preferences. About an hour.

  3. 3

    I tailor the direction

    Your words, your structure, your practice. The design decisions are mine to make.

  4. 4

    I build the identity

    Typography, colour palette and a refined typographic wordmark, made to hold together.

  5. 5

    Two revision rounds

    You review the whole site and send one consolidated list. Twice.

  6. 6

    Launch and handover

    Checked, made live, and handed over with everything you need to keep it.

You choose the overall direction. I make the detailed design decisions — you should not have to assemble a website out of fonts, colours and layout options.

What you are actually buying

Something you own, instead of something you rent.

A therapist with listings on two directories pays somewhere between £300 and £550 every year, indefinitely, for space in someone else’s website. £995 is roughly two years of that, once — and at the end of it the website is yours.

The directories still have their place. But they are rented shelf space, and a practice eventually needs a room of its own.

  • Yours to keep

    You own the finished website outright. Your domain and hosting stay in your own accounts, not mine.

  • Built properly

    Responsive, fast, and tested to WCAG 2.2 AA — because a good proportion of the people arriving are reading on a phone, or neurodivergent, or distressed.

  • Written for the rules you work under

    Professional-body designations set exactly as your body permits, and no claim on the page you cannot stand behind.
