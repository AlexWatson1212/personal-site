FAQ

Questions therapists often ask.

Is this only for therapists at the beginning of private practice?

No. What matters is not career stage but readiness. The process can support a therapist preparing to launch, refining an existing practice or replacing an online presence that no longer reflects the quality and direction of their work.

Do I need to have chosen a niche?

No. The work helps you recognise patterns in the people and therapeutic relationships that naturally suit you. The aim is useful focus, not an artificial label.

Is the logo professionally designed?

Yes. I develop the strategic and creative direction from your Practice Clarity, then a professional identity designer creates your bespoke logo system. The process includes concept development and agreed revision rounds before the final identity files are prepared.

Is the website based on a template?

No. The website is planned, designed and developed specifically around your practice. Its structure, language, visual direction and client journey grow from the strategy rather than being fitted into a pre-existing template.
