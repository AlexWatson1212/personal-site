The short version

You are good at your work. You should not have to be good at websites as well.

You bring the practice — who you work with, what you offer, how you would like it to feel. I bring the structure, the design and the build. There is no stage where you have to become a marketer.

The job is accuracy rather than persuasion. Someone should be able to tell from your website whether you are the right therapist for them — and if the answer is no, that is a good outcome too. It saves you both a first conversation that was never going to work.

How it works

The direction is where it starts. It is not what you get.

The collection removes the blank page. It does not remove the work of understanding your practice and deciding what it should say — which is most of what you are paying for.

  1. Your decision

    Choose a direction

    The one that already looks closest to the practice you want people to recognise.

  2. You

    Tell me about the practice

    A written form, about an hour. Who you work with, what you offer, your words.

  3. Your decision

    Approve how it is being tailored

    A short written note first: the palette, the structure and the decisions I have made for your practice. Nothing is built until you agree with it, and after that the design decisions are mine to make.

  4. Your decision

    Approve the finished website

    After two rounds of changes. Then it is launched, handed over, and looked after for the first year.
